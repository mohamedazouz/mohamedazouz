# Content Rules

## Voice

- First person, plain British English ("organise", "colour", "programme").
- Senior-leadership tone: people and culture first, outcomes over activity, confident but
  never boastful. Short sentences. No buzzword lists.
- Keywords (engineering leader, senior engineering manager, agentic AI, AI-driven
  development, streaming, London) must read naturally — never keyword-stuff.

## Confidentiality (REQUIRED)

Never publish:

- Internal system names, architectures, team names, metrics, traffic numbers or incidents
  from any current or past employer.
- Unreleased products, strategy, financials, or anything not already public.
- The phone number or personal Gmail address. Public email is `me@mohamedazouz.com` only.
- The resume PDF from outside the repo (it contains private contact details).

## Positioning

- The site is personal marketing only. **No "open to work", "open to roles",
  "looking for opportunities" or any job-seeking wording** — not in copy, meta or JSON-LD.
- Current employer: **Skydance** (formerly Warner Bros. Discovery — same role, new owner).
- Headline titles: "Senior Engineering Manager · Engineering Leader". Career timeline keeps
  actual held titles so it matches LinkedIn.

## Articles

- Every article must be the author's own view, with no employer-confidential detail.
- If a post touches an employer's public product, state the connection in the post.
